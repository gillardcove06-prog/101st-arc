01 — Field brief

What the unit is

The 101st Advanced Recon Commandos is a player community for Helldivers 2. It is run by Commander Maximus. This website is a front door for that community. It is not an official Arrowhead Game Studios site, and it is not endorsed by the developers.

Members come here to find reliable teammates: people who mark a patrol before they throw a stratagem, who talk in plain language, and who would rather extract together than chase a highlight. Bring a microphone if you have one, and a bit of patience for the rest of the squad.

Nothing on this page is an in-game rank, a studio announcement, or a record of service. It is a fan community with a military-sci-fi name, full stop.

  • Recon first

    Read the ground before you rush the objective. A short look saves a long reinforce.

  • Squad over glory

    Call the play, cover the flank, and leave with the people you dropped with.

  • Straight talk

    Clear callouts beat noise. Friendly tone, no drama, no pretending this is official.

02 — Enlistment

How to join

Joining happens in Discord. Use the button on this page. It goes to the 101st server and nowhere else.

  1. 1

    Open the invite

    Use the Join the Discord button. It goes to discord.gg/hpKS4ktKYc. Do not follow a lookalike link that claims to be the 101st.

  2. 2

    Read the rules and say hello

    Read #rules, then introduce yourself in #welcome. A mic is not required.

  3. 3

    Pick a class and a company

    In #class-and-company-selection, pick a class and FOX or BEAR. Classes are Assault, Medic, Support, Marksman, Tanker, Demolitions, Flame Trooper, Gas Trooper, Stealth, and Engineer.

  4. 4

    Join the clan and find a squad

    Run /clanjoin. Squads are in #looking-for-group. Ping @LFG.

Lore

The Merak Legacy

The doctrine of the 101st was forged through experience. The 101st Advanced Recon Commandos were founded by veterans of the First Legion during the March to Cyberstan. Their defining battle occurred on their homeworld of Merak, where the veterans encountered the Jet Brigade and Flame Corps in some of the most brutal fighting they had ever experienced. With Gunship Fabricators unable to be destroyed, enemy air power remained a constant threat while the Flame Corps turned the battlefield into an unrelenting assault of fire and destruction. Casualties reached levels never before experienced by the veterans of the First Legion. Those who survived Merak became the foundation of the 101st.

From Merak came the central lesson of the Commandos: "A force that cannot adapt will eventually be overwhelmed."
